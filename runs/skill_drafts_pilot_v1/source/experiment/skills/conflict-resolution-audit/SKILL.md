---
name: conflict-resolution-audit
description: Form and verify an answer from conflicting or revised records by checking supporting sources, revision chains, and concrete counterevidence.
---

Answer using the supplied authority and revision rules. First identify the information that supports the requested result, including any corrections, replacements, withdrawals, or reinstatements that affect it. Follow each explicit reference to its actual target; apply a revision only if it remains valid under those rules. Mere disagreement does not cancel an item.

Construct a candidate answer from the resulting effective information, preserving scope, order where relevant, and the distinction between an absolute state and a change to that state.

Then perform a separate evidence check:
- For information used, verify that its source is authorized and no effective revision invalidates it.
- For relevant information excluded, verify the specific rule or effective revision that excludes it; a later withdrawal may itself have been withdrawn.
- Check whether any valid item was omitted, applied twice, or given the wrong direction, units, or scope.
- For long records, reconcile intermediate results and independently check the final combination. Agreement about which items are valid does not by itself verify the calculation.

Change the candidate answer only when the check identifies a concrete discrepancy. Do not invent an ambiguity or cancellation. If a material conflict remains unresolved under the supplied rules, state it within the requested format. Return the answer in that format without needing to narrate this check.

POLICY MEMO - Sep 1
Launch dates for Atlas program projects are set by the Program Office. Messages from Engineering or Vendor Relations are informational and never change a launch date. A record counts only if its author's office held this authority on the date the record was issued. A later authorized record replaces earlier ones. A retraction restores the value that the retracted record replaced.
